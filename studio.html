Booking terms

Simple, and in writing.

Payment

  • Your booking is confirmed once it's paid in full.
  • Pay by PayNow or card when you book. PayNow slots are held for 12 hours while your payment comes through.
  • No GST. The rate you see is the rate you pay.

Reschedule & cancellation

  • Reschedule or cancel for free up to 48 hours before your start time.
  • Inside 48 hours, or if you don't show, the payment is forfeited.

Your session

  • Sessions run 1 to 4 hours, 9am to 9pm, seven days a week. Book at least 24 hours ahead.
  • We reset the studio for an hour between bookings, so your set is dressed and lit when you arrive.
  • Raw footage within 24 hours. Two rounds of revisions on every edit.
